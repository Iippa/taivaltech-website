Prototyping & Testing

Turn uncertainty into measurable decisions quickly.

I help teams design and run lean prototype loops that answer the right technical questions, not just produce another build.

Typical scope

Prototype strategy, test matrix definition, fixture and setup guidance, data interpretation, failure analysis, and iteration prioritization across mechanical and electromechanical interfaces.

Best fit

Teams preparing first pilot units, validating redesigns after field issues, or scaling from PoC behavior to product-grade repeatability.

Prototype loop

Each loop must close one meaningful uncertainty. Otherwise it's noise.

Define hypothesis

What exactly are we trying to prove or disprove in this build?

Build minimum useful prototype

Use the lightest setup that can still produce trustworthy signal.

Run and analyze

Capture data and failure behavior against explicit acceptance criteria.

Decide next move

Choose to proceed, redesign, or isolate a narrower test path based on evidence.

If your prototypes keep producing mixed signals, I can help tighten the loop.
