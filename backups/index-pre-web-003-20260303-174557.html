The problem

I see this repeatedly: hardware startups don’t run out of ideas. They run out of senior engineering time at the worst moments — design reviews, supplier negotiations, or the week before a pilot run.

Large consultancies are often slow to engage and expensive to keep. Too often, the senior engineer sells the project and a junior ends up doing the work.

Independent freelancers move faster, but deep mechanical and system-level experience is rare. I’ve seen few people who have designed a drive unit from scratch, solved DFM failures at a contract manufacturer, and carried a first production run through.

That’s the gap I fill: senior judgment, hands-on, when it counts.
