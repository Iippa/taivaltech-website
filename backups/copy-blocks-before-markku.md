## Problem
- Label: The problem
- I see this repeatedly: hardware startups don’t run out of ideas. They run out of senior engineering time at the worst moments — design reviews, supplier negotiations, or the week before a pilot run.
- Large consultancies are often slow to engage and expensive to keep. Too often, the senior engineer sells the project and a junior ends up doing the work.
- Independent freelancers move faster, but deep mechanical and system-level experience is rare. I’ve seen few people who have designed a drive unit from scratch, solved DFM failures at a contract manufacturer, and carried a first production run through.
- That’s the gap I fill: senior judgment, hands-on, when it counts.

## Services
- Label: Services
- H2: Three ways we can work together.

### One-time — Mechanical Design & Review
- I can take hands-on CAD ownership or run a focused external review — structural, thermal, or electromechanical. You get either detailed design output or a written report with clear findings and prioritized actions.
- For teams that need senior mechanical capacity now, or an independent read before committing to tooling.

### Days to weeks — Prototyping & Testing
- Structured prototyping and testing for electromechanical systems — from first PoC to functional validation. Covers build strategy, test plan, failure analysis, and iteration guidance.
- For teams building first pilot units or validating a design before it scales.

### 1–3 months — NPI & First Production
- End-to-end support through new product introduction — pre-production validation, pilot batch, DFM, supplier coordination, process definition, and factory-floor issue resolution.
- For teams launching their first or second hardware product and going through production for the first time.
