The 10x engineer angle, explained

I don't mean magic productivity. I mean removing bottlenecks at the right moment.

Most hardware teams are blocked by senior decisions, not junior effort. Tolerance stack-ups, supplier choices, test strategy, and pilot build trade-offs can burn weeks if the person making the call hasn't seen the same failure mode before.

My role is to shorten that cycle: identify the highest-risk decision early, make it explicit, and move the team forward with documented choices. That's where the real leverage comes from — not working 10x longer, but deciding better and earlier.
