The problem

Hardware startups don’t run out of ideas. They run out of senior engineering time at the worst possible moments — design reviews, supplier negotiations, the week before a pilot production run.

Large consultancies are slow to engage and expensive to keep. When they do show up, the senior engineer presents the pitch. A junior does the work.

Freelancers are faster, but deep mechanical and system-level experience is rare. Most have breadth. Few have designed a drive unit from scratch, resolved a DFM failure at a contract manufacturer, and survived a first production run.

That’s the gap. Not engineering in general — senior judgment, available when it counts.
